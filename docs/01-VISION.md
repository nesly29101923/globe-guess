# 01. Vision

## Why this game exists

TrendForge posts automatic short videos. The trivia channel (map and flag quizzes) is the one that went viral:
single videos pass 1 M views, about 20 % of viewers are in the United States, the rest is global. Paid installs
are too expensive for a new game, so the videos are our install channel. The game exists to convert that
attention into players, and players into ad and purchase revenue.

## The funnel, and what it demands from the game

1. Someone watches "WHAT COUNTRY IS THIS?", guesses along for 60 seconds, and wants their score.
2. They see the game in the video's end card, caption, pinned comment or profile link, and install it.
3. **The first thing they see must be the video they just watched, playable.** Same title card, same map
   look, same purple highlighted country, same 3-2-1 ring and tick, same green reveal. The first question is
   on screen within about 5 seconds of the first launch: no login, no tutorial pages, no permission pop-ups.
4. They finish a round, get a score they can share (the videos already ask "comment your score"), and are
   pulled into round two by a reward and a visible next goal.
5. They come back tomorrow for the Daily Challenge and their streak.

Every design decision is judged against this funnel. If a feature delays step 3 or weakens step 5, it waits.

## Audience

- Casual, all ages, phone held in one hand, sessions of 2 to 5 minutes, often with sound off.
- They are here for the "I know this one!" feeling and for proving they are good at geography. They enjoy
  learning the answer when they miss.
- Mixed skill: some can name every country in Africa, most know about 40 countries. The game must feel good for
  both, which is what the five difficulty tiers from the studio are for.
- US players matter most for revenue, so US states is a first-class topic, not an extra.

## Pillars

1. **It is the video, playable.** Recognisable in one glance by someone who came from the channel.
2. **Every tap feels good.** Instant, loud (visually), generous feedback. See `04-FEEL-AND-UI.md`.
3. **Always one more round.** Each result screen shows a reward just earned and a goal that is close.
4. **A reason to return every day.** Daily Challenge, streak, daily rewards, missions.
5. **You are getting somewhere.** A collection (the Atlas) and a journey across the world that fill up.
6. **Fair.** Skill decides the result. Money and ads buy comfort and speed, never answers the player can't
   earn. No dark patterns.

## What it is not (version 1)

No accounts, no real-time multiplayer, no chat, no user-made quizzes, no server. A deterministic Daily
Challenge and local leaderboards of your own bests are enough to launch. Online leaderboards and friend
challenges are the first things to add once retention is proven.

## The loop back to the videos

The game also feeds the channel: the share card after a round is a 9:16 image in the video's style with the
score and the game's name, and the Daily Challenge result is shareable as a short emoji grid. Players posting
their scores is free promotion on the same platforms the videos live on.
