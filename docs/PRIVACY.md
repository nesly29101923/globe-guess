# Privacy policy: Globe Guess

_Last updated: [DATE]. Replace [COMPANY], [EMAIL] and [DATE] before publishing._

Globe Guess ("the game") is made by [COMPANY]. This policy explains what data the game uses.

**No account.** The game has no sign-in. Your progress (coins, levels, streak, settings) is stored only on your device.

**Advertising.** The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP
address and information about how ads are shown and used, to serve and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK
and Switzerland you are asked for consent first; on iOS you are asked for permission to track. You can change your
choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

**Purchases.** In-app purchases are handled by Apple or Google and processed through RevenueCat, which receives an
anonymous user id and the purchase record. We never see your payment details.

**Analytics.** The game records anonymous gameplay events (for example rounds played and questions answered) to improve
the game, through Google Firebase Analytics. They are not linked to your name or contact details.

**Notifications.** Reminders are scheduled on your device only, and only if you allow them.

**Children.** The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.
